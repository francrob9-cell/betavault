# Case Study: Cognitive Bias Atlas Game

## Challenge

Cognitive bias education often relies on static charts and memorization. That format can be informative, but it does not always create durable learning or curiosity.

## Approach

I turned a cognitive bias atlas into an interactive 3D prototype. Players explore a procedural world, discover bias crystals, wash away plaque-like clusters, collect biases, build structures, and move between layers.

## What The App Demonstrates

- Rapid interactive learning prototype development
- Three.js scene building
- Collision and movement systems
- Procedural terrain chunks
- Collection and progression mechanics
- Translating abstract concepts into spatial gameplay

## Business Value

The prototype shows how training content can become a more immersive learning product. It could support education, leadership training, team decision-making workshops, or interactive museum-style explainers.

## Future Roadmap
