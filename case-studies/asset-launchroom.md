# Case Study: Unfound Door Asset Launchroom

## Challenge

Creative delivery can become messy when clients receive raw folders, oversized media, mixed asset types, and too many internal controls. The review experience should be simple, visual, and focused.

## Approach

I built a client-facing asset launchroom that separates internal asset management from client review. The client portal strips away unnecessary navigation and focuses on images, video previews, review, and lightweight social-format actions.

## What The App Demonstrates

- Client portal UX
- Media library organization
- Thumbnail-first asset browsing
- Asset-only review mode
- Lightweight crop and posting workflow concepts
- Product thinking around delivery, review, and approval

## Business Value

The app makes media delivery feel more professional and reduces friction between creative teams and clients. It demonstrates how hosted assets can become a polished client experience rather than a file transfer.

## Future Roadmap

- Cloud asset storage
- Secure client links
- Approval and revision states
- Video transcoding and preview generation
- Social platform publishing integrations
