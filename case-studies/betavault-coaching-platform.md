# Case Study: BetaVault Coaching Platform

## Challenge

Athlete development workflows are usually fragmented across text messages, spreadsheets, video files, and informal notes. That makes it hard for coaches to track goals, review video, understand training context, and keep athletes engaged over time.

## Approach

I built a coach/athlete portal concept that centralizes profile data, goals, training sessions, video review, notifications, and onboarding. The app separates athlete-facing mobile workflows from coach/admin review workflows and models the platform around role-based access.

## What The App Demonstrates

- Translating a real operational workflow into a usable app
- Role-based product design for coach and athlete experiences
- Firebase-style auth, Firestore, and Storage data modeling
- Video review UX with timestamped comments and frame annotation
- Goal comments, milestone tracking, and AI recommendation concepts
- Mobile-first thinking for athlete uploads and session logging

## Business Value

The app reduces scattered communication, creates a persistent training history, and gives coaches a clearer priority queue. It also creates a foundation for AI-assisted training recommendations while keeping coach approval in the loop.

## Future Roadmap

- Production Firebase Security Rules
- Server-side notification provider
- AI-generated milestone suggestions
- Coach approval workflow for recommendations
- Analytics around training load, fatigue, and progress
