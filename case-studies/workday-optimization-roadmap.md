# Case Study: Workday Optimization Roadmap

## Challenge

Large enterprise optimization efforts often begin as long lists of tasks. Without a decision layer, executives struggle to see what matters, what depends on what, where risk sits, and which decisions need attention.

## Approach

I built an executive-facing roadmap app that turns Workday optimization data into a searchable, visual planning tool. It links task context to Jira and Smartsheet-style records, supports board-packet export, and gives leadership a clearer view of owners, priorities, schedule windows, and impact.

## What The App Demonstrates

- Strategic IT roadmap design
- Executive communication and decision support
- Jira/Smartsheet integration thinking
- Dependency mapping and schedule window framing
- Board-ready PDF export
- Plain-English search and context summarization

## Business Value

The app reframes a task list as a governance tool. It helps leadership understand tradeoffs, sequence work, identify decision points, and communicate priorities across departments.

## Future Roadmap

- Server-side Jira and Smartsheet API integration
- Saved scenarios and change history
- Risk heatmap by month/workstream
- Owner accountability reporting
- Automated board packet generation
